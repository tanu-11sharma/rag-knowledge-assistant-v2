TITLE: Billing & Plans
We offer three plans: Free, Pro, and Enterprise.
The Free plan includes 1,000 API calls per month and community support.
The Pro plan costs 49 dollars per month and includes 100,000 API calls plus email support.
The Enterprise plan is custom-priced, includes unlimited API calls, a dedicated account manager, and a 99.9 percent uptime SLA.
Invoices are issued monthly and can be downloaded from the Billing tab as a PDF.
Downgrading a plan takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle, not immediately.
